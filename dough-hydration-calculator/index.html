The Science of Baker's Percentages & True Hydration

Why professional artisan bakers never use standard recipe cups, teaspoons, or naive starter calculations.

📐 Why Flour is Always 100%

In domestic recipes, percentages add up to 100%. In baking, Total Flour Weight is always defined as 100.0%, and every other ingredient is expressed as a ratio relative to that flour.

This universal standard enables effortless batch scaling: whether mixing a single 450g boule or an industrial 200kg batch, multiplying your flour weight by the baker's percentage yields the exact gram requirement for every ingredient.

💧 The Sourdough "Starter Trap"

A 100% hydration sourdough starter is 50% flour and 50% water by weight. If you aim for 75% hydration and add 20% starter without subtracting its internal water, your effective dough hydration actually jumps to nearly 78%.

SpinFoundry's True Hydration engine computes the exact flour ($FlourFromStarter = \frac{\text{Starter}}{1 + H_s}$) and water ($WaterFromStarter = \frac{\text{Starter} \times H_s}{1 + H_s}$) inside your levain, ensuring your dough never collapses from accidental excess water.
