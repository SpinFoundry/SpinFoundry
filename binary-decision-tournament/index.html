Why Binary Duels Defeat Decision Paralysis

The prefrontal cortex struggles when tasked with simultaneously comparing 10 or more options across multiple implicit dimensions (effort, impact, urgency, fun). By constraining decisions to isolated, rapid-fire binary comparisons (« A or B? »), cognitive load collapses to near zero. Instinctual preferences take over, producing clear, mathematically sound prioritizations without executive fatigue.
